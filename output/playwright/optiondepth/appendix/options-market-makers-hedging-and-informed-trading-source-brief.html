How It Was Used

  • To justify modeling dealer hedging as an influence on underlying price behavior while still treating hedge pass-through as less than certain.
  • To support quote-aware trade classification instead of relying on raw trade price alone.
  • To motivate an explicit calibration layer for effective dealer inventory rather than assuming every unit of inferred customer inventory becomes full mechanical hedge flow.

Paraphrased Takeaways Used In The Docs

  • Option market maker hedging and informed trading interact, which means the tape reflects both inventory adjustment and informed demand.
  • Trade classification quality matters when turning public prints into directional flow proxies.
  • The presence of dealer hedging effects does not imply a one-for-one mapping from inferred option inventory to realized underlying movement.
