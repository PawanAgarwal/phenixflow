Does 0DTE Options Trading Increase Volatility?

Canonical source: SSRN abstract 4426358

URL: https://ssrn.com/abstract=4426358

Local purpose: preserve the paper provenance used in the OptionDepth reconstruction docs.

Access note: the original SSRN PDF and abstract page were blocked by SSRN Cloudflare verification from this API environment on 2026-03-15, so this local PDF is a provenance brief rather than the publisher PDF.

How It Was Used

  • To justify treating 0DTE exposure as a meaningful intraday volatility input rather than a negligible edge case.
  • To motivate backtests that separate 0DTE-heavy sessions from ordinary expiry structure.
  • To support the claim that next-session EOD heatmaps should be stress-tested most aggressively on index products with concentrated same-day hedging pressure.

Paraphrased Takeaways Used In The Docs

  • The paper studies whether 0DTE options activity is associated with higher market volatility, especially in index-linked products.
  • The relevant mechanism for our use case is dealer hedging pressure near expiry, which can change realized intraday price behavior even when the underlying news flow is unchanged.
  • That makes 0DTE concentration an empirical calibration problem for our heatmap and backtest stack, not something we should ignore or assume away.
